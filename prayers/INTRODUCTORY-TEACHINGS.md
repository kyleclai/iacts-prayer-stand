## The Four Basic Truths

1. God loves us and wants us to be happy.
2. We are sinners and separated from God.
3. Jesus is the only way to the Father.
4. We must have a personal relationship with Jesus.

## Prayer

1. What is prayer?
2. Reasons for prayer (I.A.C.S.T.)
   - **I** — Invocation (Name of God and call on the Holy Spirit)
   - **A** — Adoration
   - **C** — Contrition
   - **S** — Supplication
   - **T** — Thanksgiving

## WORD

Bible Reflection — Word Sharing Circle:

- **W** — Word
- **O** — Order
- **R** — Reflection or Reality
- **D** — Direction

## LIPJASS

- **L** — Listen
- Don’t **I** — Interrupt
- Don’t **P** — Preach
- Don’t **J** — Judge
- Don’t **A** — Advise
- It should be **S** — Secret
- It should be **S** — Sacred

## Sacraments

7 Sacraments:

- Sacraments of Initiation — Baptism, Confirmation, Eucharist
- Sacraments of Healing — Penance, Anointing of the Sick
- Sacraments of Service/Mission — Holy Orders, Matrimony
