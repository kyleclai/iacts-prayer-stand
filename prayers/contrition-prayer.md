# Contrition Prayer

Loving, merciful Lord,  
I am deeply sorry for the sins I’ve committed on your beautiful creation. Yet amongst all the violence and hurt in this world, you still seek us and urge us to open our eyes/heart to you. Your son Jesus, has made the ultimate sacrifice for our salvation, forgiving us of our sins past, present, future. So may we accept you and gain to courage to unleash the invisible heavy-ladens we carry that we not speak amount, in order to dissolve to walls that lead to you.
