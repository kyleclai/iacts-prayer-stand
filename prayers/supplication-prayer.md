# Prayer of Supplication

Heavenly Father,  

We come before You in the middle of a restless season. Our hands are full, our minds are racing, and our hearts are often divided. We humble ourselves and admit that we cannot create the peace we need on our own.

Lord, we specifically ask for Your divine intervention in our stress.

Calm the storms in our thoughts and the anxieties in our chests. When the demands of this season feel like a flood, help us stand on our feet.

Grant us the grace to work with a quiet spirit. (1 Peter 3:4) Help us to see the people around us through Your eyes, rather than seeing them as interruptions. Soften our hearts and minds where exhaustion has reached.

We surrender our to-do lists and our worries to You, trusting that You, are the provider of all we need. Let Your peace, guard our hearts and our minds today and throughout this week.

We ask this through Christ our Lord.

Amen.
