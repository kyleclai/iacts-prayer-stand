# Prayer Contribution Template

Use this template to write prayers in a consistent I.A.C.T.S. format.

## The I.A.C.T.S. Prayer Framework

### I — Invocation
Begin by calling upon the Holy Trinity or the Holy Spirit. This step consciously invites God's presence into your heart, signaling the start of sacred time.

Example:
> "Come, Holy Spirit, fill my heart..."

### A — Adoration
Praise God for who He is—His majesty, love, beauty, and goodness. Focus entirely on His character rather than what He can do for you.

### C — Contrition (Confession)
Silently examine your conscience, acknowledge where you have fallen short, and ask for His mercy. This removes spiritual barriers and restores your relationship with Him.

### T — Thanksgiving
Express explicit gratitude for blessings, answers to prayer, and grace in your life. Thank Him for both big gifts and small daily gifts.

### S — Supplication
Present your specific requests and needs to God. This includes praying for your own guidance and protection, and interceding for others who are sick, suffering, or in need.

## Sample 5-Minute I.A.C.T.S. Prayer

### Invocation
“In the name of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. Come, Holy Spirit, guide my mind and quiet my heart during this time.”

### Adoration
“Lord, You are all-powerful, perfectly just, and infinitely loving. You are the creator of everything beautiful in this world.”

### Contrition
“Forgive me for the times I lost my patience today and failed to show love to those around me. Lord, have mercy.”

### Thanksgiving
“Thank You for the safety of my family, the roof over my head, and the peace You give me even during stressful times.”

### Supplication
“Please grant me wisdom for my upcoming decisions, heal my sick friend, and give strength to those who are lonely today. Amen.”
